# Table Tale v0.6.0-alpha

> **Alpha.** Table Tale is still in alpha: features, data and URLs may change between versions.

Communities now manage their shelf: every copy has an owner, games carry extra info, and the shelf can be members-only.

## What's new

- **Shelf copies with their owners.** Owners, admins and moderators edit a game on their community's shelf from the pencil button on its card. Each copy has its own row and owner: a member of the community, or the community itself. Add a copy, remove one, or change who owns it. A game keeps at least one copy; to take it off the shelf, remove the game.
- **Additional info.** Up to 500 characters per game on the shelf, such as expansions included or missing pieces. Shelf cards show the copies, their owners and the info.
- **Members-only shelf.** In the Manage tab, choose whether the shelf is visible to everyone or to members only. A members-only shelf is hidden from non-members on the community page and on the Games page. A private community's shelf is always members-only.

## Changes you'll notice

- A game's title, cover and details are shared by every community and aren't edited from a shelf.
- Existing copies are credited to whoever added the game to the shelf. Change the owners from the shelf editor.

## Fixes

- Going from Games to another page and back no longer shows broken images with their alt text; the illustrated cover shows instead.

## Known issues

- Real game covers don't show yet: they're linked from store websites that the site's security policy blocks, so the illustrated cover shows instead (#68).
