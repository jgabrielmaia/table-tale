# Table Tale v0.8.0-alpha

> **Alpha.** Table Tale is still in alpha: features, data and URLs may change between versions.

Community covers get the full width of the screen, can be repositioned, and lend their colour to the community page.

## What's new

- **Full-width cover.** The cover photo now spans the whole width of the community page.
- **Reposition the cover.** Owners and admins click **Reposition** on the cover, drag the photo up or down (or use the arrow keys), and save. The same framing is used on the community's card.
- **Edit the cover in place.** **Edit cover** opens the file picker straight away; once the photo is up, you can reposition it right there.
- **Size guidance.** While editing, a line under the cover shows the photo's size and the recommended one: **3000 × 750 px (4:1)**, PNG, JPEG or WebP up to 2 MB. Photos under 1500 px wide get a warning that they may look blurry.
- **The page takes the cover's colour.** Each community page picks the main colour of its cover and uses it for a soft glow under the cover, the active tab and a few borders. It's always adjusted to stay readable, and black-and-white or very grey covers keep the usual theme. The header, buttons and faction colours don't change.

## Fixes

- The cover is no longer narrower than the rest of the page on wide screens.
- Adding and removing co-owners now works on the live site. The database change it needed (from v0.7.0) had not been applied.
