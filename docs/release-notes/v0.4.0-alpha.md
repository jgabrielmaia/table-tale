# Table Tale v0.4.0-alpha

> **Alpha.** Table Tale is still in alpha: features, data and URLs may change between versions.

Games now live on community shelves, every card is clickable, and players get a public page.

## What's new

- **Games from nearby communities.** The Library tab is now **Games** and shows the games on the shelves of communities near you: one card per game, with the communities that have it, their copies and distance. Your location comes from the browser (or a city you pick); the radius is 5–100 km, 20 km by default, and is remembered on your device. Your own communities are always included, and private ones stay hidden from non-members. Filter by community and sort by name, distance or BGG rating.
- **A community's Games tab is the Games page for that community.** Covers, search, BGG categories and mechanics, players, duration, favourites and status, with the community's copies on each card. Owners add games, import from BoardGameGeek and remove games right there.
- **Player pages.** Clicking a member opens their public page (`/players/…`): name, level, faction, bio and Fate card. Achievements, the session diary and personal games stay private.
- **Every card is clickable.** Member and game cards open with a click anywhere on them; owner controls keep working on their own.
- **New achievement: Guardião da Estante** (*Keeper of the Shelf*). Own a community and it unlocks, with +25 XP. Owners' cards on a community page now look like an unlocked achievement, with a gold "Dono" badge.
- **The release tag shows under the logo.**

## Changes you'll notice

- **Game statuses are now Por jogar · A jogar · Já jogado** (Not played · Playing · Played) instead of "Em progresso" and "Concluído".
- **"Arcanos & Elementais" is now "História"** in Portuguese navigation (English keeps "Lore").
- **`/library` and `/biblioteca` are gone**, with no redirect: use `/games` and `/games/import`.
- "My library" is now **"Os meus jogos"** (My games), and the home page no longer shows the numbers block; its highlights only use games on a community shelf.
- The header keeps every link on one line and stays aligned at every width; below 1100px it collapses into the menu.
- Every page has its own browser tab title (the communities pages used to say "Page not found").

## Fixes

- The favourite and status controls on game cards were never visible (the card clipped them). They now sit at the bottom of each card.

## Needs setup before it works

- **Guardião da Estante** needs the migrations `20261112000001_achievement_owner_type.sql` and `20261112000002_achievement_owner.sql` applied to the database, in that order. Until then the achievement doesn't appear; the gold owner badge already works.
- The default "Table Tale" community still has no location ("Por definir"), so it doesn't appear in anyone's Games list by distance. Set its location from the community page.

## Known issues

- Signed-out visitors can't open the communities pages yet: the database refuses the member count for anonymous visitors. A fix is planned.
